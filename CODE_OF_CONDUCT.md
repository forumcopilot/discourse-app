# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## In short

We are here to build a good Discourse client. Everyone who participates —
in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussion — is expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith. Most bad code is a misunderstanding, not malice.
- Keep review about the work. Say what is wrong and why; skip the rest.
- Accept that maintainers decide what gets merged, and that "no" is a
  complete answer when it comes with a reason.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and sustained
disruption are not acceptable anywhere in the project's spaces.

## Reporting

If something happens that you do not think belongs here, email
**forumcopilot@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately and taken
seriously; you will get a response.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that do
not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently exclude anyone
whose behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

The full text of the Covenant, including the enforcement guidelines this
project adopts, is at the link above.
